Deck Board, Joist & Footing Calculator (5/4×6, 2×6 & Composite Estimator)

Planning a wood or composite deck requires coordinating two separate framing layers: the structural substructure (ledger board, 2×8 or 2×10 joists, joist hangers, double support beams, and concrete pier footings) and the surface decking layer (decking boards, drainage gaps, and hidden clips or face screws). This calculator computes both layers simultaneously and renders a scaled top-down framing diagram.

1. How to Calculate Deck Boards, Joists & Fasteners

Deck Board Rows (Along Projection) = ceil( (Projection_Ft × 12) ÷ (Board_Width_in + Gap_in) )
Total Decking Linear Feet = Deck_Board_Rows × Deck_Length_Ft
Interior + End Joists = ceil( (Deck_Length_Ft × 12) ÷ Joist_OC_in ) + 1
Face Screws Needed = Deck_Board_Rows × Joist_Count × 2 screws per crossing

3. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual width of a 5/4x6 or 2x6 deck board?

Both nominal 5/4×6 wood decking, 2×6 dimensional decking, and standard composite deck boards (Trex, TimberTech) have an actual face width of 5 1/2 inches (140 mm). When installed with a standard 3/16 inch (4.8 mm) drainage gap between boards, each row covers 5.6875 inches of deck projection.

Should deck joists be spaced at 12 inches or 16 inches on center?

Standard wood decking (5/4×6 PT pine, cedar, or 2×6) laid perpendicular to the joists uses 16-inch on-center joist spacing. However, composite or PVC decking (or any decking laid at a 45-degree diagonal angle) typically requires 12-inch on-center joist spacing to prevent bouncy underfoot flex.

How many deck screws or hidden fasteners do I need per square foot?

Each intersection where a 5 1/2 inch wide deck board crosses a joist requires 2 face screws (or 1 hidden clip plus 1 screw). At 16-inch OC joist spacing, count approximately 3.5 face screws (or 1.75 hidden clips) per square foot of deck area. Always buy 316 stainless steel or ACQ-rated coated exterior structural screws.

How many concrete footings (sonotubes) does a ledger-attached deck require?

A rectangular deck attached to the house rim joist with a flashed ledger board uses one main support beam resting on posts spaced every 6 to 8 feet along the beam length, requiring ceil(Deck_Length_Ft ÷ 7) + 1 concrete pier footings. A freestanding (floating) deck requires two beams and double the footing count.

What is the correct gap between wood vs composite deck boards?

Kiln-dried cedar or composite decking requires a 3/16 inch (5 mm) gap for water drainage and thermal expansion. Wet pressure-treated (PT) pine shrinks as it dries in the sun and should be installed tightly together (or with an 8d nail spacer, 1/8") so the gaps don't widen beyond 1/4" after seasoning.
